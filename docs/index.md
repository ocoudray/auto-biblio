# Auto Biblio Recap

An automated watch on AI research and engineering: it collects new papers, repositories,
releases, blog posts and community discussions, deduplicates them across sources, and (in
upcoming sub-projects) triages them against an interest profile to produce a weekly digest
and a living knowledge base.

**Status:** sub-project 1, *foundation and collection*, is implemented: a daily,
LLM-free collection from arXiv, Hugging Face Daily Papers, GitHub, Hacker News and RSS
feeds into a separate data repository.
